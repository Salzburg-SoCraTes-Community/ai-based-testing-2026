# Issue 01: Quantity Stepper

## Summary
Add a small quantity stepper to the ticket app.

## Scope
- Plus button
- Minus button
- Quantity display

## Acceptance Notes
- The count should start somewhere sensible.
- The minus button should not go below zero, or maybe one, depending on the product choice.
- The step size is probably 1.
- The display should update immediately.
## Known ambiguity
- Minimum count may be zero or one.
- Keyboard input behavior is not specified.
- Press-and-hold behavior is out of scope for now.
